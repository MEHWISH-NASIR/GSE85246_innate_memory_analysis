TCGA Pan-Cancer Kinase Exploration

Integrated expression, clinical and immune-infiltration analyses

Scope

This report provides a standardized TCGA pan-cancer framework for candidate kinase evaluation.

For each TCGA cancer type, analyses are included where the required data are available:

  • tumor versus normal expression
  • matched tumor-normal expression
  • early-stage tumor versus normal expression
  • stage-wise expression trends
  • grade-wise expression trends
  • prevalence of high or aberrant expression
  • survival associations as secondary evidence
  • sample-size and data-availability limitations
  • immune/inflammatory infiltration controls

The completed analysis now contains two linked modules:

  • JAK3 — the reference pan-cancer kinase analysis
  • Multikinase — comparative RNA, clinical and ATAC-RNA analysis of the expanded candidate panel

The multikinase module uses JAK3 as the completed benchmark and extends the framework to nine additional candidates emerging from the innate immune-memory analysis.

Interpretation

TCGA RNA-seq represents bulk tissue. Observed kinase expression may therefore reflect both malignant-cell biology and differences in immune or stromal cell content.

Associations that remain after adjustment for measured immune proxies are described as immune-adjusted or residual associations. They should not be interpreted as proof of malignant-cell-intrinsic expression.

Early-stage elevation is considered evidence of an association with early disease and does not establish a causal role in tumor initiation.
