# GSE166238

JAK3 post-washout validation following oxLDL training in primary human monocytes/macrophages.

## Design

- 3 donors: DA, DB, DC
- RPMI control
- oxLDL
- 24 h exposure
- stimulus removal
- 5-day recovery/differentiation
- Day 6 transcriptomic measurement

## Main JAK3 finding

No consistent JAK3 deregulation was observed.

### Day 1

- effect: -0.071
- FDR: ~1.00
- donor direction: 2 positive / 1 negative

### Day 6

- effect: -0.224
- FDR: ~1.00
- donor direction: 1 positive / 2 negative

### Trajectory

- Day6 minus Day1 treatment-effect change: -0.153
- FDR: 0.987

## Classification

**No convincing persistent JAK3 response after oxLDL training.**

This negative result is retained as part of the unbiased cross-dataset analysis.
